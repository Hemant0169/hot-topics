Article 1 - The Value of Money

Money serves as a medium of exchange, a unit of account, and a store of value. However, its perception has shifted dramatically over centuries—from gold-backed physical currencies to digital assets and central bank policies. Money's intrinsic value exists entirely through shared societal trust.

Understanding financial literacy and economic drivers helps individuals recognize how inflation and monetary policy erode purchasing power over time, emphasizing why capital allocation matters for long-term security.

Article 2 - GMO Food

Genetically Modified Organisms (GMOs) remain one of the most debated topics in modern agriculture and nutrition. Proponents highlight increased crop yields, resistance to extreme weather, and enhanced nutrient profiles that mitigate global hunger.

Conversely, critics urge caution regarding long-term ecological impacts and agricultural biodiversity. Striking a balance between scientific innovation and environmental safety is essential for sustainable global food security.

Article 3 - The View of World in 2100

By the year 2100, humanity will inhabit a vastly transformed world shaped by climate adaptation, artificial intelligence, and demographic realignments. Smart cities powered by renewable energy grids will define modern urban centers.

Global collaboration in clean energy transitions and space exploration will likely reshape international policy, ensuring sustainable civilization for generations to come.
